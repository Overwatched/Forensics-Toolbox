Okänd app

1

Identitet: paket/bundle, signerare, version

iOS

  • IPA: Payload/<App>.app/Info.plist
  • Installerad: /private/var/containers/Bundle/Application/<UUID>/<App>.app/Info.plist
  • CFBundleIdentifier, CFBundleShortVersionString, CFBundleVersion
  • embedded.mobileprovision (dev/ad hoc/enterprise) eller App Store-kvitto

Android

  • /data/app/<paket>-*/base.apk (+ split-APK)
  • AndroidManifest.xml: package, versionName, versionCode
  • /data/system/packages.xml — installer (com.android.vending vs sideload)
  • Signatur v1/v2/v3 på APK:n

Hash Calculator · Magic Text · iLEAPP · ALEAPP

2

Behörigheter

iOS

  • *UsageDescription i Info.plist
  • Entitlements i signaturen
  • TCC: /private/var/mobile/Library/TCC/TCC.db — client = bundle-ID

Android

  • uses-permission i manifestet
  • /data/system/users/0/runtime-permissions.xml
  • /data/system/appops.xml
3

Privat lagring + WAL

iOS

  • /private/var/mobile/Containers/Data/Application/<UUID>/
  • Documents, Library, tmp — backup-domän AppDomain-<bundle>
  • *.sqlite tillsammans med -wal och -shm

Android

  • /data/user/0/<paket>/ (eller /data/data/<paket>/)
  • databases, shared_prefs, files — *.db + -wal -shm
  • Delad lagring: /sdcard/Android/data/<paket>/

4

Nätverk och konton

iOS

  • Keychain: internetlösenord och generiska poster för bundle-ID
  • Library/Cookies/Cookies.binarycookies, WebKit-cache
  • Konton: /private/var/mobile/Library/Accounts/Accounts3.sqlite

iOS Keychain

Android

  • shared_prefs — tokens, endpoints
  • Konton: /data/system_de/0/accounts_de.db och /data/system_ce/0/accounts_ce.db
  • WebView-cookies under app_webview/
5

Residual: aktivitet, notiser, foton

iOS

  • KnowledgeC, Biome App.InFocus, interactionC
  • Notiser: /private/var/mobile/Library/UserNotifications/
  • Foton: ZCREATORBUNDLEID / ZEDITORBUNDLEID

Queries · Photos.sqlite

Android

  • UsageStats: /data/system/usagestats/ (ofta XML)
  • Digital Wellbeing: …/com.google.android.apps.wellbeing/databases/app_usage
  • MediaStore owner_package_name
  • Notiser är OEM-specifika — ALEAPP
